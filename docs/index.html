Rewind Privacy Policy

Rewind is designed for personal photo and video memories. The app does not operate its own server, and your media content is not uploaded to a developer-owned server by default.

1. Information Rewind may access

  • Photos and videos you allow through the iOS Photos permission.
  • Media metadata provided by iOS, such as creation date, location metadata, album membership, media type, and local asset identifiers.
  • Collections, memory notes, preferences, quota state, app settings, and other information you create inside Rewind.
  • Purchase and subscription entitlement status provided by Apple StoreKit when you buy or restore Rewind Pro.
  • iCloud sync data if you choose to enable iCloud-based features.

2. How the information is used

Rewind uses information to show, organize, filter, and revisit your photos and videos; create timeline, location, collection, and note-based experiences; remember app settings; enforce free and Pro limits; restore purchases; and sync supported app data across your Apple devices when iCloud sync is enabled.

3. Photos, videos, and local processing

Photo and video browsing, filtering, random memory playback, and collection organization are primarily processed on your device. Rewind does not intentionally copy your full photo or video library to a developer server.

4. iCloud sync

If iCloud sync is enabled, supported app data may be stored in and synchronized through your Apple iCloud account. Apple provides the infrastructure for iCloud, and you can manage it through iOS, iCloud, and Apple ID settings.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Rewind Pro purchases, renewals, cancellations, refunds, and payment processing are handled by Apple App Store and StoreKit. The developer does not receive your full payment card information.

6. Third-party services

Rewind may use Apple system frameworks and services such as Photos, StoreKit, iCloud, and MapKit. The current version does not include a third-party advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK for cross-app tracking.

7. Diagnostics and support

If you contact the developer for support, information you provide, such as messages, screenshots, diagnostic logs, device details, or reproduction steps, may be used to investigate and fix issues.

8. Data retention and deletion

You can revoke permissions in iOS Settings, delete Rewind to remove local app data, and manage iCloud-synced data through Apple ID and iCloud settings.

9. Children

Rewind is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as features, legal requirements, or App Store review requirements change. For privacy questions, contact: https://github.com/VoryKwin/RewindPages/issues

回瞬隐私政策

回瞬是一款用于回顾、整理和记录个人照片与视频记忆的 iOS 应用。应用没有自建服务器，你的媒体内容默认不会上传到开发者拥有的服务器。

1. 回瞬可能访问的信息

  • 你通过 iOS 照片权限允许应用访问的照片和视频。
  • iOS 提供的媒体元数据，例如创建时间、位置元数据、相册归属、媒体类型和本地资源标识符。
  • 你在回瞬内创建的收藏册、回瞬记录、偏好设置、额度状态、应用设置和其他应用内数据。
  • 你购买或恢复回瞬 Pro 时，由 Apple StoreKit 提供的购买和订阅权益状态。
  • 如果你选择开启 iCloud 同步，相关同步数据可能会通过你的 Apple iCloud 账户处理。

2. 信息的使用方式

回瞬使用这些信息来展示、整理、筛选和回顾你的照片与视频；生成时间线、地点、收藏册和记录相关体验；记住应用设置；处理免费版和 Pro 功能限制；恢复购买；并在你开启 iCloud 同步时同步支持的数据。

3. 照片、视频和本地处理

照片与视频浏览、筛选、随机回顾和收藏整理主要在你的设备上完成。回瞬不会有意把你的完整照片或视频资料库复制到开发者服务器。

4. iCloud 同步

如果开启 iCloud 同步，支持的应用数据可能会存储并同步到你的 Apple iCloud 账户。iCloud 的基础设施由 Apple 提供，你可以在 iOS、iCloud 和 Apple ID 设置中管理相关数据。

5. 购买与订阅

回瞬 Pro 的购买、订阅续费、取消订阅、退款和付款处理由 Apple App Store 与 StoreKit 完成。开发者不会获得你的完整支付卡信息。

6. 第三方服务

回瞬可能使用 Apple 提供的系统框架和服务，例如 Photos、StoreKit、iCloud 和 MapKit。当前版本不包含第三方广告 SDK，也不使用第三方分析 SDK 进行跨 App 跟踪。

7. 诊断与支持

如果你联系开发者寻求支持，你提供的消息、截图、诊断日志、设备信息或复现步骤可能会用于定位和修复问题。

8. 数据保留与删除

你可以在 iOS 设置中撤销权限，删除回瞬以移除本地应用数据，并通过 Apple ID 和 iCloud 设置管理 iCloud 同步数据。

9. 儿童隐私

回瞬不面向 13 岁以下儿童，开发者也不会有意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息。

10. 政策更新与联系

当功能、法律要求或 App Store 审核要求发生变化时，本政策可能会更新。如有隐私疑问，请联系：https://github.com/VoryKwin/RewindPages/issues
