Rewind Privacy Policy

Rewind is designed for personal photo and video memories. The app does not operate its own server, and your media content is not uploaded to a developer-owned server by default.

1. Information Rewind may access

Rewind may access or store the following information only as needed to provide app features:

  • Photos and videos you allow the app to access through the iOS Photos permission.
  • Media metadata provided by iOS, such as creation date, location metadata, album membership, media type, and local asset identifiers.
  • Collections, memory notes, preferences, quota state, app settings, and other information you create inside Rewind.
  • Purchase and subscription entitlement status provided by Apple StoreKit when you buy or restore Rewind Pro.
  • iCloud sync data if you choose to enable iCloud-based features.

2. How the information is used

Rewind uses information to show, organize, filter, and revisit your photos and videos; create timeline, location, collection, and note-based experiences; remember your app settings; enforce free and Pro feature limits; restore purchases; and sync supported app data across your Apple devices when iCloud sync is enabled.

3. Photos, videos, and local processing

Photo and video browsing, filtering, random memory playback, and collection organization are primarily processed on your device. Rewind does not intentionally copy your full photo or video library to a developer server. If you share screenshots, logs, or media with the developer for support, that information is provided by you voluntarily.

4. iCloud sync

If iCloud sync is enabled, supported app data may be stored in and synchronized through your Apple iCloud account. Apple provides the infrastructure for iCloud. You can manage iCloud availability and related data through iOS, iCloud, and Apple ID settings.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Rewind Pro purchases, subscription renewals, cancellations, refunds, and payment processing are handled by Apple App Store and StoreKit. The developer does not receive your full payment card information. The app may read entitlement status so it can unlock Pro features and restore purchases.

6. Third-party services

Rewind may use Apple system frameworks and services such as Photos, StoreKit, iCloud, and MapKit. The current version does not include a third-party advertising SDK and does not use a third-party analytics SDK to track your activity across other apps or websites.

7. Diagnostics and support

If you contact the developer for support, the information you provide, such as messages, screenshots, diagnostic logs, device details, or reproduction steps, may be used to investigate and fix issues. Please avoid sending personal content that you do not want reviewed for support purposes.

8. Data retention and deletion

You can revoke Photos, location, and other permissions in iOS Settings. You can delete Rewind to remove locally stored app data from your device. Data synchronized through iCloud may need to be managed through your Apple ID and iCloud settings.

9. Children

Rewind is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, legal requirements, or App Store review requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date when appropriate.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through GitHub Issues: https://github.com/VoryKwin/RewindPages/issues

回瞬隐私政策

回瞬是一款用于回顾、整理和记录个人照片与视频记忆的 iOS 应用。应用没有自建服务器，你的媒体内容默认不会上传到开发者拥有的服务器。

1. 回瞬可能访问的信息

为了提供应用功能，回瞬可能会在必要范围内访问或保存以下信息：

  • 你通过 iOS 照片权限允许应用访问的照片和视频。
  • iOS 提供的媒体元数据，例如创建时间、位置元数据、相册归属、媒体类型和本地资源标识符。
  • 你在回瞬内创建的收藏册、回瞬记录、偏好设置、额度状态、应用设置和其他应用内数据。
  • 你购买或恢复回瞬 Pro 时，由 Apple StoreKit 提供的购买和订阅权益状态。
  • 如果你选择开启 iCloud 同步，相关的同步数据可能会通过你的 Apple iCloud 账户处理。

2. 信息的使用方式

回瞬使用这些信息来展示、整理、筛选和回顾你的照片与视频；生成时间线、地点、收藏册和记录相关体验；记住你的应用设置；处理免费版和 Pro 功能限制；恢复购买；并在你开启 iCloud 同步时，在你的 Apple 设备之间同步支持的数据。

3. 照片、视频和本地处理

照片与视频浏览、筛选、随机回顾和收藏整理主要在你的设备上完成。回瞬不会有意把你的完整照片或视频资料库复制到开发者服务器。如果你为了反馈问题主动向开发者发送截图、日志或媒体内容，这些信息属于你主动提供。

4. iCloud 同步

如果开启 iCloud 同步，支持的应用数据可能会存储并同步到你的 Apple iCloud 账户。iCloud 的基础设施由 Apple 提供。你可以在 iOS、iCloud 和 Apple ID 设置中管理 iCloud 可用性及相关数据。

5. 购买与订阅

回瞬 Pro 的购买、订阅续费、取消订阅、退款和付款处理由 Apple App Store 与 StoreKit 完成。开发者不会获得你的完整支付卡信息。应用可能读取权益状态，用于解锁 Pro 功能和恢复购买。

6. 第三方服务

回瞬可能使用 Apple 提供的系统框架和服务，例如 Photos、StoreKit、iCloud 和 MapKit。当前版本不包含第三方广告 SDK，也不使用第三方分析 SDK 来跟踪你在其他 App 或网站中的活动。

7. 诊断与支持

如果你联系开发者寻求支持，你提供的消息、截图、诊断日志、设备信息或复现步骤可能会用于定位和修复问题。请不要发送你不希望被用于支持排查的个人内容。

8. 数据保留与删除

你可以在 iOS 设置中撤销照片、位置和其他权限。你可以删除回瞬，以移除设备上本地保存的应用数据。通过 iCloud 同步的数据可能需要通过 Apple ID 和 iCloud 设置管理。

9. 儿童隐私

回瞬不面向 13 岁以下儿童，开发者也不会有意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息。

10. 政策更新

当应用功能、法律要求或 App Store 审核要求发生变化时，本政策可能会更新。更新后的版本会发布在本页面，并在适当情况下标注新的生效日期。

11. 联系方式

如对隐私政策有疑问，请通过 GitHub Issues 联系开发者：https://github.com/VoryKwin/RewindPages/issues
